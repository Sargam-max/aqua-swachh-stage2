Firmware Telemetry & Pipeline

ADC Signal Conditioning & Filtering:

The firmware takes 30 successive analog readings on ADC1, discards highest/lowest outliers, and applies an Exponential Moving Average (EMA, $\alpha=0.25$) to eliminate switching power supply ripple.

Interrupt-Driven Flow Metering:

The YF-S201 turbine triggers hardware ISR on GPIO 5 falling edges. Every 1,000 ms, frequency is converted via the turbine characteristic: Q = pulses / 7.5 L/min.

Serial Bridge Logging:

Transmits 4-tuple CSV packets over USB UART at 115200 baud directly to data/data_logging_esp32.py for local CSV persistence and real-time inference.

Stage 2 ML Engineering & Ground-Truth Protocol

Essential guidelines addressing reviewer expectations for Stage 2 of atomQUEST: distinguishing a validated prototype classifier from ungrounded drinking water potability or RUL days.

1 Where do real ground-truth labels come from?

True labels must be anchored to independently known physical cartridge states, not circular rules based on raw TDS readings:

  • Normal: Logged from freshly flushed, certified RO cartridges operating under standard municipal water conditions.
  • Monitor: Logged from cartridges tested after processing 50% to 75% of rated cumulative throughput ($\Sigma Q \approx 3,000 - 4,500\text{ Liters}$).
  • Service Required: Logged from cartridges near their service limit, or cartridges subjected to controlled accelerated bench fouling (high particulate loading or scaling).

2 Why classification instead of predicting remaining useful life days?

Predicting remaining filter days without multi-month longitudinal field data across varying seasonal water sources is scientifically ungrounded. For Stage 2, predicting condition state (Normal, Monitor, Service Required) proves the IoT pipeline and feature engineering work with transparency and scientific integrity.

3 Physics Feature Engineering & Mathematical Formulation

Salt Rejection (η): η = 1 - (TDS_out / TDS_in)

Measures solute rejection across polyamide membrane independent of feed fluctuations.

Temperature Compensation: TDS_norm = TDS / (1 + 0.02*(T-25))

Corrects electrical conductivity drift (~2% per °C) across summer and winter.

Flow-Rejection Indicator: Index = Flow * max(η, 0)

Isolates particulate head-loss (flow drop) from membrane rupture (rejection drop).
